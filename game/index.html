What kind of game is Scamming Granny?

Scamming Granny: Call Chaos is a single-player comedy call center simulator for Android, developed by Mad Axe Studios. Its fictional scam-call premise combines conversation puzzles, a walkable low-poly office, coworker interactions and workplace slapstick. It is a game of made-up conversations, rather than a real calling service.

Win the conversation

Type your own English dialogue or use optional voice input. Read each character’s reactions, earn their trust and solve fictional code challenges.

Survive office life

Meet coworkers, complete missions, manage your night shift and sneak a break while your boss patrols the office.

Find your next distraction

Explore the night street, play paper and computer minigames, buy upgrades and continue into freeplay after the campaign.

Questions before your first shift

Does Scamming Granny make real phone calls?

No. Every call and character is fictional and contained inside the game. The game does not contact real people, read your contacts or ask for real banking details, passwords or verification codes.

Is it multiplayer?

No. Scamming Granny: Call Chaos is a single-player game. Coworkers and callers are game-controlled characters.

Can I play offline?

The core campaign and local dialogue system work offline. Ads, online services and some device speech-recognition services need an internet connection.

Do I need a microphone?

No. You can type your call dialogue. Voice input is optional and asks for microphone permission when used. Your Android speech service may process audio online; see the privacy policy for details.

Which languages can I use?

Interface and story text support English, Arabic, Bengali, Simplified Chinese, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Japanese, Nepali, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and Spanish. Call dialogue input and word puzzles require English.

Does it use an online AI chatbot?

No. The game’s conversations use a local, rule-based dialogue system. Characters respond to game rules, rather than an external generative AI service.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

The game is supported by ads, including optional rewarded ads. It has no in-app purchases. Game currency is fictional and cannot be cashed out.

Is this a horror Granny game?

No. Scamming Granny: Call Chaos is an original workplace comedy and simulation game about fictional phone conversations. Its tone is office chaos and slapstick.

Where can I download it?

The Android release is being prepared for Google Play. Watch the gameplay preview above for a look at the game.

Keep the chaos fictional. This is a satirical game, not advice for real-world scams. Never share personal details, banking credentials or real verification codes with an unexpected caller.
