Privacy Policy

Scamming Granny: Call Center Chaos (Android app com.madaxe.sohan.granny)

Developer: madaxe. Effective date: 18 November 2026.

The short version. Scamming Granny is a comedy parody game. Every company, person and verification code in it is fictional. You do not need an account and the game never asks for your name, email, phone number or payment details. What you type or say on in-game calls stays on your phone. We use Google services for game analytics, crash reports, settings updates, optional notifications and ads, and those services collect device and usage data as described below.

1. Who we are

This game is made by madaxe ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, email dhungel9814@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

  • Save game and settings. Your progress, money, upgrades and settings (sound, motion, vibration, reminders) are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
  • What you type on calls. Lines you type to the in-game characters are processed by the game on your device. We do not send them to any server and we do not include them in analytics or crash reports.
  • Microphone (optional). If you tap Mic on a call, the game asks for microphone permission and uses your phone's built-in Android speech recognition to turn your voice into text. The game does not record or store audio. Speech recognition is provided by your device's speech service (usually Google), which may process the audio under its own privacy policy. You can deny or revoke the permission at any time and keep playing by typing.
  • Photos (optional). The in-game Webcam app can save a picture of your in-game character to your phone's gallery. It does not use your real camera. Saved pictures stay on your device.
  • Daily reminders (optional). Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

The game uses the following Google services. They automatically collect some information from your device when you play. We do not sell this information.

4. Ads and your choices

  • Where ads appear. Ads are shown at natural breaks (for example after a call result or at the end of a night, and when you open or return to the game). Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus.
  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, the game shows Google's consent form before personalized ads are used. You can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy and ad choices in the game.
  • Personalized ads. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

5. Permissions the app may ask for

  • Internet / network state: for analytics, crash reports, settings updates and ads.
  • Notifications: for optional reminders and game news.
  • Microphone: only if you use voice on calls (see section 2).
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): only to save an in-game picture to your gallery.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads service as described in section 4.

6. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete the game or clear its data. Data held by Google services is kept according to their retention settings: for example, Google Analytics event data is kept for up to 14 months, and Crashlytics crash reports for up to 90 days.

7. Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to processing. Because the game has no accounts, the identifiers involved are device and installation IDs. To make a request, email dhungel9814@gmail.com; we will respond within 30 days. You can also delete all local data at any time by uninstalling the game, and reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.

8. Children

This game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, email us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. We do not run our own servers that receive your gameplay or personal information.

10. About the game's content

Scamming Granny is satire. It does not teach, support or enable real fraud, and it never asks you for real codes, passwords or financial information. If someone contacts you in real life asking for a verification code, do not share it.

11. Changes to this policy
